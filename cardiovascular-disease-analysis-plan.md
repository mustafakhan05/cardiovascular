# CARDIOVASCULAR DISEASE ANALYSIS - PROJECT PLAN

## 1. PROJECT TITLE

Analyzing Cardiovascular Disease Data

## 2. PROJECT GOAL

The main goal of this project is to study patient data and understand which factors may be linked to cardiovascular disease (CVD). We will also use the data to explore whether a patient has cardiovascular disease or does not have it.

## 3. DATASET

The dataset contains information about 70,000 patient records. It includes:

- Age
- Height
- Weight
- Gender
- Systolic blood pressure
- Diastolic blood pressure
- Cholesterol level
- Glucose level
- Smoking habits
- Alcohol use
- Physical activity
- Cardiovascular disease status

## 4. TOOLS

We will use:

- Python
- Pandas to read and work with data
- NumPy for calculations
- Matplotlib and Seaborn to make charts

## 5. PROJECT STEPS

### Step 1: Load the Data

Open the patient dataset in Python and check how many rows and columns it contains.

### Step 2: Understand the Data

Look at the columns, values, and types of information. Check how many patients have and do not have cardiovascular disease.

### Step 3: Study Patient Information

Compare age, height, weight, and gender. Study smoking, alcohol use, and physical activity.

### Step 4: Check Health Factors

Study blood pressure, cholesterol, and glucose levels. Look for patterns between these factors and cardiovascular disease.

### Step 5: Calculate BMI

Calculate Body Mass Index (BMI) using weight and height.

BMI = weight in kilograms / (height in meters x height in meters)

Compare BMI values between different patient groups.

### Step 6: Clean the Data

Find incorrect or unusual values. Remove records with diastolic pressure higher than systolic pressure. Also remove extreme height and weight values, as described in the notebook.

### Step 7: Make Charts

Create charts to understand the data. Use bar charts, violin plots, and heatmaps. These charts can help show patterns and relationships.

### Step 8: Study Relationships

Use correlation to check which features are related. Compare Pearson and Spearman correlation results.

### Step 9: Study Age and Disease

Compare the number of patients with and without cardiovascular disease at different ages.

### Step 10: Review the Results

Write down the main findings. Explain which factors show a relationship with cardiovascular disease in this dataset.

## 6. EXPECTED RESULT

At the end of the project, we should have:

- A better understanding of the patient data.
- Cleaned data for analysis.
- Charts showing important patterns.
- Information about relationships between health factors and CVD.
- A short report explaining the results.

## 7. CONCLUSION

This project uses data analysis to study cardiovascular disease. It helps us explore how age, blood pressure, cholesterol, glucose, smoking, and other factors are related to CVD. The results show patterns in this dataset. They do not prove that a factor causes the disease and are not a medical diagnosis.
